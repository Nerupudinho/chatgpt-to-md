# Privacy Policy

**ChatGPT to Markdown** is a Chrome extension that exports ChatGPT conversations to Markdown files. This policy describes how the extension handles your data.

## Data Collection

**This extension does not collect, transmit, or store any user data.**

## What the Extension Does

1. **Reads page content**: When you click the download button on a ChatGPT page, the extension reads the conversation from the page DOM (the visible conversation on screen).

2. **Converts locally**: The conversation text is converted to Markdown format entirely within your browser.

3. **Saves to your device**: The Markdown file is downloaded directly to your computer using your browser's standard download functionality.

## What the Extension Does NOT Do

- Does not send any data to external servers
- Does not collect analytics or usage statistics
- Does not use cookies or local storage
- Does not track your browsing activity
- Does not access conversations beyond the currently open page
- Does not require any user accounts or logins
- Does not display advertisements

## Permissions

The extension requires access to `chatgpt.com` and `chat.openai.com` to inject the download button and read conversation content. It does not access any other websites.

## Third Parties

This extension does not share data with any third parties because it does not collect or transmit any data.

## Open Source

This extension is open source. You can review the complete source code at:
